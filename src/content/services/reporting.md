The question most brands cannot answer after a campaign is whether it reached
anyone new. Follower counts and impressions will not tell you: a post that
recirculates an existing audience and a post that finds a new one can look
identical in a summary.

We report non-follower reach separately for exactly that reason. It is the
number that says whether a partnership did the job organic posting could not.
