Gifted collaborations have a reputation for producing nothing, and usually the
reason is structural: nothing was written down, so nothing was owed.

We run gifting on the same footing as paid work. The agreement names the
deliverable, the format and the window. If a creator is not prepared to commit
to that, we find out before you send anything.
