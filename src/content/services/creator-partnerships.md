Most brands do not have a creator problem. They have a *which creator* problem,
and no reliable way to answer it. We keep our own database of creators we have
worked with or vetted, so a shortlist starts from evidence rather than from a
hashtag search.

Every partnership is covered by a written agreement setting out deliverables,
usage rights and timings before anything is posted. That is the step most
gifted collaborations skip, and it is the reason they so often produce nothing.
